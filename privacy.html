StudySync Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

StudySync is a personal software project that synchronizes academic assignment information with Google Calendar.

Information StudySync Uses

StudySync processes academic assignment information such as assignment titles, due dates, and course information. It also accesses Google Calendar as authorized by the user.

How Google Calendar Data Is Used

Google Calendar access is used to create, locate, and update calendar events associated with StudySync.

Google Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Authentication

StudySync uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization system to access Google Calendar. Authentication credentials are stored locally for the authorized user and are not published by StudySync.

Data Sharing

StudySync does not sell personal information or Google user data. StudySync does not intentionally share Google Calendar data with third parties.

Data Retention

StudySync stores authentication information locally as necessary to maintain authorized access to Google Calendar. Calendar events created by StudySync remain in the user's Google Calendar unless removed by the user or by future StudySync functionality explicitly authorized by the user.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke StudySync's access to their Google Account through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the developer through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
